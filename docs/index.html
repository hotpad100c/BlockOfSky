A frame of the sky block

天空方块的一帧

The sky block is a window to the sky: whatever is behind it, even solid stone, you see the sky through it. The scene below is a closed stone room lit by glowstone. Its far wall has a window made of sky blocks with three blocks of solid stone right behind it; a stone block and a light blue glass block stand in front of the window.

天空方块就像一扇通向天空的窗：哪怕它后面是实心的石头，看过去也是天空。下面的场景是一个封闭的石头房间，用荧石照明。远处墙上有一扇由天空方块组成的窗，窗的正后方是三格厚的石头；窗前放了一块石头和一块浅蓝玻璃。

Without a shader pack, the mod photographs the vanilla sky into a cube and maps it onto the block's faces. With a shader pack, the mod makes the pack treat the block's pixels as sky, and the pack draws its own sky there. The flowchart switches between the three cases; every picture is a real buffer from the same frame of the smoke test.

不开光影时，模组把原版天空拍成一个立方体，再贴到方块的面上。开着光影时，模组让光影把方块所在的像素当成天空，由光影自己画出天空。下面的流程图可以在三种情况之间切换，每张图都是冒烟测试里同一帧的真实缓冲。

Why let the pack draw the block again为什么让光影自己再画一遍

A shader pack's vertex shaders may move vertices anywhere. Kappa draws the whole scene into the lower-left 75% and scales it up later (TAAU), with sub-pixel TAA jitter every frame. Working out where the block is with vanilla's matrices would miss where the pack actually drew it, and the sky would drift off the block. So the mod draws the block again with the vanilla entity render type: Iris swaps in the pack's own program, so the scaling and jitter happen as well. The pixels whose depth changed are exactly where the block is, without knowing what the pack did to the vertices.

光影的顶点着色器可以随意移动顶点。Kappa 先把整个场景画进左下角 75% 的区域，再放大回全屏（TAAU），每帧还有亚像素的 TAA 抖动。如果按原版矩阵自己算方块在哪，就会和光影实际画的位置对不上，天空会偏离方块。所以模组用原版的实体渲染类型把方块再画一遍：Iris 会把它换成光影自己的程序，缩放和抖动都照样发生。比较画前画后的深度，变了的像素就是方块真正所在的地方，不需要知道光影做了什么变换。

Two Iris 1.8 pitfallsIris 1.8 的两个坑

  • While it renders the level, Iris skips every draw with a shader it does not know (allowUnknownShaders is off by default). The depth-copy and hole shaders are exempted with SkipList.NONE_FORCE.
  • Applying a shader Iris does not know binds the pack's framebuffer, and clearing it binds the main one. So the full-screen passes call apply() first, then bind their own framebuffer, then draw.
  • Iris 在渲染世界期间会跳过它不认识的着色器（allowUnknownShaders 默认关闭）。复制深度和挖洞的着色器通过 SkipList.NONE_FORCE 豁免。
  • 应用一个陌生着色器时，Iris 会切到光影的帧缓冲，清除时又切回主帧缓冲。所以全屏通道先 apply()，再绑定自己的帧缓冲，然后才绘制。

Reproduce重现

On the render-notes branch, run ./gradlew runSmokeClient -PsmokeTag=bsl. Screenshots go to run/smoke/screenshots and the buffers of each stage to run/smoke/captures/<tag>. To test a shader pack, put it into run/smoke/shaderpacks and enable it in run/smoke/config/iris.properties.

在 render-notes 分支运行 ./gradlew runSmokeClient -PsmokeTag=bsl。截图保存在 run/smoke/screenshots，各阶段的缓冲保存在 run/smoke/captures/<tag>。要测试某个光影包，把它放进 run/smoke/shaderpacks，再在 run/smoke/config/iris.properties 里启用它。
